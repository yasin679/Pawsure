# Pawsure pitch video: script

Minimal style: warm ivory background, dark ink type, one sage accent, real app screenshots.
About 70 seconds, 1920 × 1080. Voice-over and music are generated with ElevenLabs.
The source is the PRD v1.5 short version (19 Nov 2026).

| # | On screen | Voice-over | Source in PRD |
|---|-----------|------------|---------------|
| 1 | Pawsure logo · “Be sure, whoever’s caring.” | Pawsure. Be sure, whoever’s caring. | Promise |
| 2 | “You leave home. Your pet stays.” | Every working day, owners leave their dog alone for two to eight hours, and trust a helper to drop in. There’s no plan, no signal when a visit is missed, and no easy backup. | §2 Problem |
| 3 | “36 of 45 owners leave their pet alone. 7 came home to something wrong.” | In our survey, thirty-six of forty-five owners leave their pet alone. Seven came home to something wrong. | §2 Survey (n = 45, exploratory) |
| 4 | “Know who’s caring, every day.” + welcome screen | Other apps sell you a caretaker. Pawsure verifies the one you already have. | §1 Positioning |
| 5 | 01 · Set up: Pawly sign-up chat | Sign-up is a short chat with Pawly. Your pet, their routine, your helper, and today’s tasks are ready. | §6.5 Sign-up with Pawly |
| 6 | 02 · Your helper: WhatsApp invite + reminder | Your helper joins on WhatsApp. Nothing to install, in her own language. Pawsure reminds her when each task is due. | §3 Sunita, §5 Helper on WhatsApp |
| 7 | 03 · Proof: bowl photo + Home | She sends a photo of the bowl. Never her face. It lands on your Home, with an honest status. | §5 Trust, §6.1 Confidence |
| 8 | 04 · You decide: review + approved | You check it, and approve it in one tap. | §6.5 Owner reviews the helper’s photo |
| 9 | 05 · Find cover: Pawly + nearby friends | And when your help can’t come, Pawly finds friends nearby who are free. Nothing is sent until you approve. | §6.3 Friend Finder |
| 10 | “Every day, at a glance.” + owner Home | Know whether planned care actually happened, and what to do when it didn’t. | §1 Core promise |
| 11 | Vertical logo · “Piloting in Mumbai, Andheri to Powai.” | Pawsure. Piloting now in Mumbai. Be sure, whoever’s caring. | §1 Pilot |

Infographics: a day timeline (2–8 hours alone, a lunch visit marked “?”), dot grids for the survey (36 of 45, 7 of 45), the three task statuses (Confirmed · Likely · Not confirmed), the order Pawly asks in (your friends → nearest, free now → you approve), and the pilot (Mumbai, Andheri–Powai · 20 homes · 4 weeks).

Voice: ElevenLabs v4, “Sharanya” (warm Indian English).

Music: soft, minimal, warm piano and light plucked strings, about 80 BPM, hopeful, no vocals; kept low under the voice.
