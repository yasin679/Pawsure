# Support runbook

Channels: helper replies “help” on WhatsApp (creates a callback case), owners use the in-app support link, anyone can call 8 AM–10 PM IST. Staff use the operations console only; they never see raw phone numbers, photos or addresses there.

| Situation | What to do | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Owner: “my helper’s check-in didn’t show” | Console → Signals for that household; explain the evidence label (Likely / Not confirmed). Late evidence within 3 h is kept and marked late. | Change a task’s evidence by hand |
| Owner: “I didn’t get an alert” | Service health → Recent deliveries (masked). If a fallback or failure shows, say so plainly. | Promise alerts will always arrive |
| Helper: “why are my points on hold?” | Rewards holds: give the reason; decide within 7 days with a note they can read. A hold is not a finding against them. | Call it misconduct or rank helpers |
| Helper wants to stop | Ask them to reply “stop”; nothing new is recorded. Past records follow retention. | Ask the owner first |
| Anyone: “delete my data” / “my data” | WhatsApp or app request creates a rights case; privacy lead replies within 7 days. | Delete records others rely on (incidents) before the retention period |
| Pet hurt / emergency | Tell them to call their vet or the 24×7 clinic on the emergency card; the S1 call chain is already running. | Give medical advice or promise a response time |
| Friend no-show or late cancel | Console → Friend Finder; the backup escalation has resumed. Offer to help the owner search again. | Share the friend’s number |
| Abuse report | Block is immediate; review within 24 h; record the outcome on the case. | Discuss one user with another |

Every console view is logged. Close cases with a short reason.
