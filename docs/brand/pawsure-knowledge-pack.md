# Part 1. Brand and social brief

## 1.1 Pawsure in one minute

- **Name:** Pawsure
- **Tagline:** *Be sure, whoever’s caring* (no full stop when used as a line on its own)
- **What it is:** a mobile app that shows pet parents honest evidence that planned pet care actually happened at home today, and helps them find trusted cover when their usual helper can’t come.
- **One-line pitch:** Other apps help you book care. Pawsure assures it happened, and has a backup when it doesn’t.
- **Positioning line:** Other apps sell you a caretaker. Pawsure verifies the one you already have.
- **Category:** pet-care assurance. It brings visibility, accountability and continuity of care.
- **Platform:** a mobile app for the owner. Helpers use WhatsApp (or a free missed call on a basic phone), with nothing to install.
- **Where:** a pilot is planned in Mumbai (Andheri–Powai): 20 homes, 4 weeks.
- **Built by:** Group-G, IITR (Apurva, Nikita, Neha, Rakesh, Sukanta, Tanvi, Vibhor, Yasin).
- **Stage:** working demo; pilot not yet run. Never describe Pawsure as launched or as having users, reviews or results.

## 1.2 Facts: what content may and may not say

**Safe to say**
- Pawsure works with the helper you already have: your maid, a family member or a walker.
- Your helper needs no app. She gets reminders and checks in on WhatsApp, in her own language (Hindi, Marathi or English in the demo), or with a free missed call.
- She sends a photo of the task (the bowl, the walk, the medicine). Never her face.
- Each task gets an honest status: **Confirmed**, **Likely** or **Not confirmed**. Pawsure never says “failed”.
- You review her photo and approve the task in one tap.
- When your help can’t come, **Pawly**, the AI assistant, finds your own friends nearby who are free. **Nothing is sent until you approve.**
- Alerts come only when something slips.
- The research figures in section 1.4, always with their source wording (“from our primary research and survey of 45 pet parents”).

**Never say or imply**
- That Pawsure guarantees a pet’s safety, that a visit is “100% verified”, or that pets can’t come to harm.
- That Pawsure is a vet, gives medical advice or is an emergency service. (“Pawsure is a coordination and evidence tool. It is not a vet or an emergency service.”)
- That it uses cameras, GPS tracking of the helper, or face photos.
- That it books or pays carers, day cares or walkers. Not at launch.
- That it has launched, has a number of users, ratings, testimonials or partners. None exist yet.
- That the helper is being watched or policed. The tone toward helpers is respect and credit, never suspicion.
- Invented statistics. Use only the numbers in section 1.4.

## 1.3 The problem

Working pet parents leave their dog alone for 2 to 8 hours and rely on a helper or neighbour. There are three gaps every working day:

1. **No shared plan.** Instructions are scattered across WhatsApp chats and memory.
2. **No signal.** Nothing tells you a task was done, or that a visit was missed.
3. **No backup.** When the helper can’t come, there is no plan B.

The story hook we use: *It’s 1:07 PM. Do you know if your dog has eaten?* The owner texts the helper, waits, and two hours later gets “haan didi sab done 👍”. Done when? Done what?

Cameras watch but can’t act. Booking apps confirm a booking, not that care happened.

## 1.4 Research (use exactly as written)

Source wording: **“From our primary research and survey of 45 pet parents (exploratory).”**

| Finding | Number |
|---|---|

| Have changed their plans because of their pet | **69%** (31 of 45) |
| Rely on someone else to check on their pet | **62%** (28 of 45) |
| Use a pet camera | **42%** (19 of 45) |

## 1.5 Who it’s for (personas, illustrative)

- **Neha, 34. Primary owner.** Out 8–10 hours a day. Her helper Sunita feeds Bruno, her dog, at lunch. Her worry: *“Is Bruno okay?”* She needs one honest status for the day and alerts only when something slips.
- **Sunita, 45. The helper.** Works across three flats. Uses WhatsApp and voice notes in Hindi or Marathi, sometimes a basic phone. Her worry: *“Will I be blamed?”* She needs a simple request in her language, no app, fair credit, and the right to stop any time.
- **Rohan and Ankita. New to the city.** No family or friends nearby. They need a trusted care circle in their own society.

**Who it is not for:** homes where someone is always in; cats with self-feeders.

The emotional jobs: feel calm and connected, no guilt about being away, trust without micromanaging, be a responsible pet parent.

## 1.6 How it works (the first day)

1. **Sign up with Pawly, your personal AI assistant.** A short chat: your name, your home, your pet, what they eat and when. Each answer becomes one of today’s tasks.
2. **Invite your helper.** She gets a WhatsApp message in her language, sends a photo of her ID (only the Pawsure team sees it), and joins with one tap.
3. **She gets a reminder at task time.** For example: “🐾 Coco, ⏰ Breakfast (8:00 AM). When done, send a photo or a short video of the bowl. No faces.”
4. **She sends a photo of the task.** The photo appears on the task on your Home, and the status changes to **Approve**.
5. **You approve in one tap.** The task moves to Completed. “Approved: Breakfast. Thank you, Lata!”

**When help can’t come:** ask Pawly in plain words (“Can someone feed Bruno at 6 PM?”). Pawly suggests your own friends nearby who are free, nearest first, and drafts the WhatsApp message. You approve every message.

**Launch features (P0 only; don’t mention later phases):**
- Pet profile and daily routine, set once by chat (with vet, allergies, medicines and an emergency card).
- Helper check-ins on WhatsApp or a free missed call.
- Proof of care with smart alerts: Confirmed, Likely or Not confirmed per task.
- AI Friend Finder for cover.
- **Not at launch:** hardware, mandatory cameras, cash payouts, vet diagnosis.

**Task types and their proof photo:** meal (the food bowl), water (the fresh water bowl), walk (the dog on its lead), medicine (the tablet strip), pee pad (the clean pad), litter (the clean tray), other (the pet).

## 1.8 Brand voice

- **Warm, calm, honest.** We reassure without overpromising. “Likely” is a feature: we tell you how sure we are.
- **Plain and short.** Everyday words, short sentences, one idea per line. Write the way a kind, sensible neighbour talks.
- **Respectful to helpers.** Helpers are partners who get credit, never suspects. Celebrate them.
- **Indian and specific.** Mumbai societies, lifts, WhatsApp groups, didi and tai, chai breaks, monsoon walks. Hinglish touches are welcome in moderation (“sab done 👍”).
- **Light humour, never at the pet’s or the helper’s expense.**
- **Headlines without full stops.** Sentence case. No ALL CAPS shouting.
- **Emoji:** a few, purposeful: 🐾 ⏰ 📷 ✅ 🐶 🐱 💛. Not a row of them.

# Part 2. Business requirements (BRD)

*Compiled from the PRD v1.5 (19 Nov 2026) and the Group-G pitch deck. Every money figure is a planning assumption, not traction.*

## 2.1 Business problem

Working pet parents leave pets alone for 2–8 hours and depend on a helper, neighbour or family member, with no plan, no signal when a check-in is missed, no history and no easy backup. Existing options solve slices: marketplaces book care, cameras watch, friends help informally. None confirm that planned care happened or provide a backup when it doesn’t.

## 2.2 Business objectives

1. Prove that owners will rely on honest daily evidence of care from the helper they already have (the habit).
2. Prove that helpers will take part voluntarily on WhatsApp or basic phones.
3. Provide a trusted backup through the AI Friend Finder.
4. Reduce checking calls and surface missed tasks fast.
5. Prove a paid model (Plus, later commissions) before scaling.

**North star:** covered care days per household per month. A day counts when every planned task is Confirmed, Likely, or matched by the owner’s return check, or a missed task was escalated and handled. Trusted-mode days count only when the owner taps “Covered” (no tap = Unknown). Counted per pet per day (IST). Days with no tasks are excluded. An escalation still open at midnight means the day is not covered. Verified care days are the quality metric under it.

## 2.3 Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|---|---|
| Primary owner (e.g. Neha) | Know care happened; calm, fewer calls |
| Co-owner / family | Shared view of the same home |
| Helper (e.g. Sunita) | No app, own language, fair credit, no blame, can stop any time |
| Backup / care circle | Asked only when a task is at risk; clear handover (key, gate) |
| Friends | Asked only with the owner’s approval; set their own availability |
| Pawsure ops team | Pilot rota 8 AM–10 PM IST, 7 days; support and escalations |
| Partners (later) | Vets, pet stores, day cares (P1), verified carers (P2) |
| Regulators | DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025; WhatsApp business policy |

## 2.4 Market and positioning

- **Positioning:** “Other apps sell you a caretaker; we verify the one you already have.”
- **Pilot market:** Mumbai, Andheri–Powai, 20 homes, 4 weeks.
- **Not our user:** homes where someone is always in; cats with self-feeders.
- **Research (n = 45, exploratory):** 36/45 leave the pet alone, 28/45 rely on someone to check, 31/45 changed plans because of the pet, 19/45 use a camera, 18/45 paid for care, 7/45 came home to something wrong. Reviewers split 3 (own-helper proof) vs 5 (outside care) of 8, which is why both live in one product.

**Competitor landscape (from the deck)**

| Provider | Type | Market | Key limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| PetBacker | Care marketplace | India + global | Quality and availability vary by provider |
| PawSpace | Home boarding | Bengaluru | Limited coverage; unclear pricing |
| ThePetNest | Pet-services app | India | Local service delivery still unproven |
| Rover / Wag! | Sitting and walks | US | Fees and consistency; US only |
| TrustedHousesitters | Sitting exchange | Global | Match timing; no backup if plans fail |
| Furbo / Petcube | Pet cameras | Global | Can watch, but can’t physically help |
| Friends and helpers | Informal care | Everywhere | No shared plan, proof or fallback |

**How Pawsure is different:** a booking confirmation then silence → proof of care; instructions scattered across chats → one shared routine; helpers must install an app → WhatsApp or IVR in their language; no fallback → cover continuity (AI Friend Finder and escalation); cameras watch but can’t act → exception-only alerts, each with a human next step.

## 2.5 Scope by release

| Path | What the owner gets | Price | Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| Own helper | Helper checks in on WhatsApp or missed call; Confirmed / Likely / Not confirmed per task; alert only if something slips | Free | P0 |
| Trusted helper mode | No check-ins, alerts or points for that helper; health, emergency card and cover stay | Free | P0 |
| AI Friend Finder | Plain-language request; own friends first, nearest first; owner approves every message. Nearby-member fallback built but off until ID checks are approved | Free | P0 |
| Graded day care | Audited on a published 100-point standard; condition cards; live score | Pay per stay | P1 (~month 3) |
| Verified carer | Verified → Trained → Trusted, earned from real visits | Pay per visit | P2 (~month 6) |

**Not in P0:** hardware, gate-app integration, cash payouts, day-care or carer booking, vet advice, background tracking, a general-purpose AI chatbot on WhatsApp.

## 2.6 Business model (assumptions)

- Free core. **Plus** at ₹149/month or ₹39/day (adds depth, never basic safety).
- 12% commission on bookings (P1/P2). Day-care software ~₹999/month.
- Coupons capped at ₹5 per household until a brand signs. Partner revenue counted as zero.

| Model (monthly) | Base | With fixes | Lean pilot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Households | 600 | 1,000 | 200 |
| Contribution / household | ~₹37 | ~₹70 | ~₹70 |
| Operating result | −₹2.78 lakh | −₹1.90 lakh | −₹31k (−₹41k without day-care software) |
| Break-even households | ~8,055 | ~3,692 | ~639 (~781 without) |

**P0-only view** (no bookings, no day-care software, no partner money), per household per month:

| | Pessimistic | Base | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue (Plus + day pass) | ₹20.75 | ₹34.05 | ₹47.35 |
| Variable cost (WhatsApp, IVR, rewards, AI, hosting, support, fees) | ₹53.41 | ₹28.76 | ₹17.45 |
| Contribution | −₹32.66 | ₹5.29 | ₹29.90 |

**Reality check:** booking commission is about half of modelled revenue but starts only in P1/P2; in P0, revenue is ~₹34–42 per household. Pilot ~₹1.5–2 lakh; one cluster with a paid team ~₹20 lakh to break-even (~month 17); payback ~22 months at 5,000 households.

## 2.7 Growth strategy (deck) and go-to-market ideas

- **0 → 1, prove the habit:** 20 homes in one cluster; ≥ 50% week-4 retention.
- **1 → 10, replicate clusters:** 3–5 nearby societies; champions and helper referrals.
- **10 → 100, build local supply:** Mumbai-wide clusters; day cares and verified carers.
- **Growth loop:** more owners → more helpers and friends → better local cover → stronger trust → more referrals.
- **Scale-up path:** Year 1 Mumbai (~1,000 users) → Year 2 four cities → Year 3 eight cities.
- **Proposed GTM plays (untested):** let the helpers sell it (one helper, three homes); society takeover (residents’ WhatsApp group, QR code in every lift, Sunday paw parade); a weekly shareable care card; a pet-parent perk with offices in Powai and an emergency card at partner vets.

## 2.8 Success measures and launch gates

**Pilot targets:** covered days 80%; helper check-in median under 30 s; 70% of helpers check in within 3 days of consent; false-confirmation rate 5% or lower; Friend Finder fulfilment 50%; week-4 retention 50%.

| Gate / test | Pass | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| Helper acceptance (10 helpers) | ≥ half accept a method | Rethink the caretaker side |
| Day-2 helper usability | ≥ 50% check in alone | Lead with calls and voice notes |
| 20-home pilot | ≥ 60% visits Confirmed/Likely; ≥ 50% active in week 4; ≥ 80% covered days | Stop and rethink |
| Friend Finder | ≥ 50% of requests answered in 15 min; zero privacy leaks | Rework density / block rollout |
| Plus pre-order (3 societies) | ≥ 20% pre-order | Rethink price or value |
| Day-care interviews (15) | ≥ 5 accept an audit | Drop or rework P1 |
| IVR and WhatsApp quotes | ≤ ₹0.50/call; ≤ ₹10/household/month total | Favour free signals; batch prompts |
| Partners (3 vets, 2 stores); legal check | Written terms; counsel sign-off | Remove benefits from pitch; no grades published |

## 2.9 Risks and open decisions

| Risk | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Helper refuses all methods | Never forced; Trusted mode; visits show Not confirmed; helper test before build |
| Thin friend network | Launch inside dense societies; honest no-match state; fallback only after opt-in |
| Privacy leak (empty homes, keys) | Coarse location, minimum disclosure, expiring details, red-team before launch |
| Big marketplace enters | Own-helper core and neutral grading; speed inside clusters |
| Nobody can cover | Cover continuity flow; honest cover-not-found state; must-happen tasks reach the whole circle; rate tracked against P1 timing |

**Still open:** SOS on Home or only under Me (now under Me); backup setting on by default; code card screen (the food-box code card was removed from the helper flow in the demo); same-society verification rule; reminder timing for helpers; advisory source; nearby ID-check provider; vendor quotes; counsel sign-off on retention and DPDP; Plus bundle; Andheri–Powai market size; carer insurance.

**Recommendation (PRD):** ship the own-helper path and AI Friend Finder together in one dense Mumbai cluster, measure covered care days, then add graded day cares and verified carers only after supply, safety and unit economics are proven.

## 2.10 Operations

Pilot ops rota 8 AM–10 PM IST, 7 days; automated alerts 24×7. S1 cases watched within 15 minutes in staffed hours; support replies within 2 hours; abuse cases within 24 hours; reward disputes and rights requests within 7 days; vendor outage switched to the fallback channel within 1 hour. Retention: photos and voice notes 90 days, call logs 12 months, incident records 3 years (counsel to sign).

## 3.2 Confidence rules (rule set v1, calibrated in the pilot)

Pawsure records whether someone came (the visit) separately from whether each task was done (the task). Calls or a gate entry show a visit, never that Bruno was fed.

| Evidence in the task window | Task state |
|---|---|
| Photo with today’s code + arrival/exit calls (long enough) or gate entry: two independent channels | Confirmed |
| Photos with the random start and end codes, each within 10 min of its call, + calls of at least the minimum duration | Confirmed |
| One task signal: photo with or without the code, voice note naming the task, or IVR code after the task prompt | Likely |
| Arrival + exit calls only, no task evidence | Not confirmed (“visit only”) |
| One call only, too short, wrong code, contradictory, or nothing | Not confirmed (never “failed”) |
| Owner’s return check matches | Matched (counts for a covered day, no points) |

**Anti-fake layers:** cross-check signals; fresh 3-digit codes at arrival and exit (a printed monthly card is optional); random photos (1 in 10, or 1 in 5 with no gate app); owner return check. They raise the effort to fake a visit; they don’t eliminate it. One mismatch holds that visit’s points and is treated as a possible honest error. Task statuses also cover reassigned, cancelled, partly done and skipped.

## 3.3 Escalation (defaults, configurable per task)

| Time (for a 4:00 PM task) | Step |
|---|---|
| 4:30 | Owner alerted with the last confirmed evidence |
| 5:00 | Backup gets a 15-minute look-in link and the spare-key note, if the owner hasn’t responded |
| 5:15 | Pawsure suggests cover (friends in P0; carers and day cares later) |

| Severity | Who is told | If nobody acknowledges |
|---|---|---|
| S1 urgent: injury, bite, collapse, missing pet, security | Owner + backup at once, WhatsApp + phone call | Calls every 5 min × 3; at 15 min all care-circle members called; helper told to call the vet |
| S2 unwell: vomiting, not eating, limping | Owner first; backup after 15 min | Phone call at 30 min |
| S3 practical: food or water out | Owner | Backup after 30 min |

“Acknowledged” means an “I’m on it” tap or pressing 1 on the call, never just a read receipt. Pawsure provides no emergency or vet service.

## 3.4 Friend Finder guardrails

- Own friends first → opted-in, ID-checked nearby members → verified carers or day cares (later).
- Rules decide eligibility and ranking (free now, distance from society/tower, has key, comfortable with the task); the LLM parses the request, explains, drafts the message and reads replies.
- Owner approves every message; society-level location only; live location only during an accepted visit; friends set their own availability; no medical advice.
- Nearby fallback stays off by feature flag until the ID-check process is approved.
- Timers: a request expires in 15 min, one reminder at 8 min, provisional hold 10 min, search closes 30 min before the task. Friend no-show (no signal by task time + 20 min) alerts the owner and starts the backup step; a friend cancelling reopens the search; late replies get “already covered”.

## 3.5 Cover continuity: when planned care is at risk (example: a 4:00 PM task)

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| 2:30 | One reminder per visit: Yes · Can’t make it · Send someone else (basic phone: press 1 / 2 / 3). The helper can also say “can’t come” any time; the owner hears within 30 s |
| 3:15 | No reply, Can’t make it, or a friend cancelling → task At risk; owner alerted |
| 3:15–3:45 | Owner’s backup list asked automatically, in order, two at a time, 10 min per pair, key holders first (adding someone to the list is the approval). Friend Finder (owner approves each message) only before its 3:30 cutoff |
| Nobody says yes | Cover not found: message + call with I’ll handle it · Ask someone else (one-time link) · Ask my whole circle · Move or skip (if urgency allows) |
| Someone accepts | A backup’s yes is confirmed at once (owner can cancel in 10 min); a friend’s yes waits for the owner. Gate approval and key plan checklist; handling note read first |
| After 4:00 | Evidence rules as usual; the missed-task ladder still runs if nothing arrives |

- **Task urgency:** can wait (walk) · time-sensitive (meal) · must happen (medicine, water on hot days). Must-happen tasks can’t be skipped; if the owner doesn’t acknowledge in 15 min, the whole care circle is called.
- **Before leaving:** if nobody has confirmed, “I’m leaving” warns and offers: do it now, leave later, take the pet, own paid provider, ask the circle, or leave anyway (“no cover planned”, not a covered day).
- **I’m delayed:** re-plans only the extra time; asks the current caregiver first (Yes / Only until __ / Can’t), then requests the uncovered part only.
- **Newcomer:** honest message at sign-up; society invite link, pet-parent swap, invite a helper already in the building. No ungraded day-care list.
- **Honest limit:** in P0 Pawsure can’t create a carer where none exists. The cover-not-found rate is tracked; above 10% of at-risk tasks, review the cluster and the timing of P1.

## 3.6 Privacy and compliance

- Helper consent before any check-in; no face photos; pause or withdraw any time.
- No public map, flat number or empty-home schedule; access details expire and stay out of notification previews.
- DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025: clear notice, easy withdrawal, rights and grievance (≤ 90 days, pilot target 7), breach report to the Board within 72 hours; operative from 13 May 2027, built to it now.
- WhatsApp: task-specific templates only (general-purpose AI chatbots barred since 15 Jan 2026); open chat stays in the app.
- No vet line, free check-up or partner reward advertised until signed.

## 3.7 Later releases (for context only; not in social content)

- **P1 day cares:** free audit out of 100 (safety 20, health 15, staff 15, space/play 15, hygiene 15, transparency 10, owner experience 10); eight red lines → not listed; A 85+, B 70–84, C 55–69; first result private, 30 days to fix, published only with consent; live score 70% audit + 30% stay proof.
- **P2 carers:** Verified (ID, police, 2 references) → Trained (practical test, quiz, first aid) → two supervised visits → Trusted (50+ visits, 90%+ Confirmed/Likely).
